[RECIPE_001]
Title: Grilled Salmon Quinoa & Avocado Power Bowl
Category: Lunch / High Protein
Prep & Cook Time: 20 Minutes
Total Calories: 520 kcal
Macro Breakdown:
  - Protein: 42g
  - Carbohydrates: 48g
  - Dietary Fiber: 7g
  - Fats: 16g
Ingredients:
  - 180g Atlantic Salmon Fillet
  - 1 cup Cooked Organic Quinoa
  - 1/2 Medium Avocado (sliced)
  - 1 cup Steamed Broccoli Florets
  - 1 tbsp Extra Virgin Olive Oil & Lemon Pepper
Preparation Steps:
  1. Season salmon fillet with lemon pepper and sea salt.
  2. Sear on medium-high heat skillet with olive oil for 4 minutes per side.
  3. Fluff warm quinoa and place in serving bowl.
  4. Top with seared salmon, sliced avocado, and steamed broccoli florets.

[RECIPE_002]
Title: Berry Power Whey Protein Smoothie
Category: Breakfast / Pre-Workout
Prep & Cook Time: 5 Minutes
Total Calories: 380 kcal
Macro Breakdown:
  - Protein: 34g
  - Carbohydrates: 42g
  - Dietary Fiber: 6g
  - Fats: 6g
Ingredients:
  - 1 scoop (30g) Vanilla Whey Protein Isolate
  - 1 cup Frozen Wild Blueberries
  - 1/2 Organic Banana
  - 1 cup Unsweetened Almond Milk
  - 1 tbsp Chia Seeds & 1 handful Organic Baby Spinach
Preparation Steps:
  1. Add almond milk, spinach, frozen blueberries, and banana into high-speed blender.
  2. Add whey protein isolate and chia seeds on top.
  3. Blend on high for 45-60 seconds until completely smooth.

[RECIPE_003]
Title: Lean Turkey & Roasted Sweet Potato Skillet
Category: Dinner / Post-Workout
Prep & Cook Time: 25 Minutes
Total Calories: 460 kcal
Macro Breakdown:
  - Protein: 38g
  - Carbohydrates: 45g
  - Dietary Fiber: 6g
  - Fats: 12g
Ingredients:
  - 170g Lean Ground Turkey Breast (93/7)
  - 1 medium Sweet Potato (cubed)
  - 1/2 Red Bell Pepper & 1/2 Yellow Onion (diced)
  - 1 tbsp Avocado Oil, Garlic Powder, Smoked Paprika
Preparation Steps:
  1. Toss cubed sweet potatoes in avocado oil, paprika, and garlic powder; roast at 200°C for 18 minutes.
  2. Sauté diced onions and bell peppers with ground turkey breast until fully cooked.
  3. Combine roasted sweet potatoes into skillet and mix thoroughly.

[RECIPE_005]
Title: Avocado & Poached Egg Organic Sourdough Toast
Category: Breakfast / Healthy Fats
Prep & Cook Time: 10 Minutes
Total Calories: 350 kcal
Macro Breakdown:
  - Protein: 22g
  - Carbohydrates: 28g
  - Dietary Fiber: 5g
  - Fats: 14g
Ingredients:
  - 1 slice Artisanal Whole Grain Sourdough Bread
  - 1/2 Ripe Hass Avocado
  - 2 Large Organic Cage-Free Eggs
  - 1 tsp Hemp Hearts, Red Chili Flakes, Sea Salt
Preparation Steps:
  1. Toast sourdough slice until golden and crisp.
  2. Mash avocado with sea salt and spread evenly over toast.
  3. Poach eggs in simmering water with a drop of vinegar for 3 minutes.
  4. Gently place poached eggs over avocado toast, sprinkle with hemp hearts and chili flakes.
