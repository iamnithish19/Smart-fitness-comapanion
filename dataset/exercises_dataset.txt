[EXERCISE_001]
ID: EX-001
Name: Barbell Bench Press
Category: Strength Training
Primary Muscle: Chest (Pectoralis Major)
Secondary Muscles: Triceps, Anterior Deltoids
Equipment: Barbell, Bench
Difficulty: Intermediate
Recommended Sets: 4
Recommended Reps: 8 - 12
Calorie Burn: 8.5 kcal/min
Instructions: Lie flat on bench with feet firm on the floor. Grip barbell slightly wider than shoulder width. Lower bar to mid-chest with controlled motion, then press explosively upward until arms are fully extended.

[EXERCISE_002]
ID: EX-002
Name: Treadmill High Intensity Interval Run
Category: Cardio / Endurance
Primary Muscle: Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Calves
Secondary Muscles: Core, Glutes
Equipment: Treadmill
Difficulty: Advanced
Recommended Sets: 1 (25 mins)
Recommended Reps: Continuous (1 min sprint / 1 min jog intervals)
Calorie Burn: 14.2 kcal/min
Instructions: Warm up for 3 minutes at 5 km/h. Alternate between 1 minute sprint at 14 km/h (1% incline) and 1 minute recovery jog at 6 km/h for 20 minutes. Cool down for 2 minutes.

[EXERCISE_003]
ID: EX-003
Name: Bodyweight Burpees
Category: HIIT / Calisthenics
Primary Muscle: Full Body
Secondary Muscles: Quadriceps, Core, Chest, Shoulders
Equipment: None (Bodyweight)
Difficulty: Intermediate
Recommended Sets: 5
Recommended Reps: 15 - 20
Calorie Burn: 12.0 kcal/min
Instructions: Stand feet shoulder-width apart. Drop into a squat, place hands on floor, kick feet back into a push-up position. Perform push-up, jump feet back to hands, and explosively jump vertically reaching arms overhead.

[EXERCISE_004]
ID: EX-004
Name: Bodyweight Pull-Ups
Category: Strength Training
Primary Muscle: Upper Back (Latissimus Dorsi)
Secondary Muscles: Biceps, Brachialis, Core
Equipment: Pull-Up Bar
Difficulty: Intermediate
Recommended Sets: 4
Recommended Reps: 8 - 10
Calorie Burn: 9.0 kcal/min
Instructions: Hang from pull-up bar with overhand grip slightly wider than shoulders. Pull chest up toward bar until chin passes bar level. Pause briefly at top, then lower with full control to dead-hang position.

[EXERCISE_005]
ID: EX-005
Name: Mountain Climbers
Category: HIIT / Core
Primary Muscle: Abdominals (Rectus Abdominis)
Secondary Muscles: Hip Flexors, Deltoids, Quadriceps
Equipment: Exercise Mat
Difficulty: Beginner
Recommended Sets: 5
Recommended Reps: 30 seconds (continuous)
Calorie Burn: 10.5 kcal/min
Instructions: Start in high plank position with shoulders directly above wrists. Drive right knee rapidly toward chest, quickly return foot back while driving left knee toward chest in a running motion. Keep hips low.

[EXERCISE_006]
ID: EX-006
Name: Romanian Deadlift
Category: Strength / Hypertrophy
Primary Muscle: Hamstrings, Glutes
Secondary Muscles: Erector Spinae, Core, Forearms
Equipment: Barbell or Dumbbells
Difficulty: Intermediate
Recommended Sets: 4
Recommended Reps: 10 - 12
Calorie Burn: 7.8 kcal/min
Instructions: Hold barbell at hip height with soft knee bend. Hinge forward at hips, pushing glutes backward while keeping spine neutral until bar passes knees. Engage hamstrings and glutes to pull back to starting upright position.

[EXERCISE_007]
ID: EX-007
Name: Dumbbell Overhead Shoulder Press
Category: Strength Training
Primary Muscle: Deltoids (Anterior & Lateral)
Secondary Muscles: Triceps, Upper Trapezius
Equipment: Dumbbells, Adjustable Bench
Difficulty: Intermediate
Recommended Sets: 4
Recommended Reps: 10 - 12
Calorie Burn: 7.2 kcal/min
Instructions: Sit upright on bench at 90 degrees. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with palms facing forward. Press dumbbells overhead until arms are nearly straight, avoiding arching lower back. Lower back to ear level under control.

[EXERCISE_008]
ID: EX-008
Name: Speed Jump Rope
Category: Cardio / Conditioning
Primary Muscle: Calves, Cardiovascular System
Secondary Muscles: Forearms, Deltoids, Core
Equipment: Speed Jump Rope
Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate
Recommended Sets: 4
Recommended Reps: 60 - 100 jumps
Calorie Burn: 13.0 kcal/min
Instructions: Hold jump rope handles with elbows close to torso. Rotate wrists to turn rope, hopping lightly on balls of feet just high enough for rope to clear. Maintain quick, steady rhythmic breathing.

[EXERCISE_009]
ID: EX-009
Name: Goblet Squat
Category: Functional Strength
Primary Muscle: Quadriceps, Gluteus Maximus
Secondary Muscles: Core, Hamstrings, Calves
Equipment: Kettlebell or Heavy Dumbbell
Difficulty: Beginner
Recommended Sets: 4
Recommended Reps: 12 - 15
Calorie Burn: 8.0 kcal/min
Instructions: Hold kettlebell vertically close to chest with both hands. Stand feet slightly wider than shoulder width. Squat down deep by driving hips back and elbows between knees. Press back up through heels to full extension.

[EXERCISE_0010]
ID: EX-010
Name: Plank to Push-Up
Category: Core & Stability
Primary Muscle: Core, Triceps
Secondary Muscles: Chest, Anterior Deltoids
Equipment: Exercise Mat
Difficulty: Intermediate
Recommended Sets: 3
Recommended Reps: 12 - 16 reps (alternating lead arms)
Calorie Burn: 9.5 kcal/min
Instructions: Start in forearm plank position. Press up on right hand, then left hand into high push-up position. Lower right forearm back down, followed by left forearm. Maintain stable hips without excessive swaying.
